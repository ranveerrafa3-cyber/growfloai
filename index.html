Questions

Before you fill
anything in

If it isn't answered here, ask on the call.

What does it cost?

It depends on your trade, your market and how hard you want to push. We price it on the call once we've seen your numbers — and if it isn't going to pay for itself, we'll tell you then rather than take the money.

Am I locked into a long contract?

We work in 90-day cycles — long enough for the ads to season, short enough that you're never trapped. If we're not hitting the estimate target we agreed, you're free to walk at the end of the cycle.

Who pays for the ad spend?

You do, straight to the platform. It stays your ad account, your data and your audience — no markup. Our fee is separate, and it's the only thing you pay us.

What if I already run ads, or have an agency?

Most contractors who come to us have a folder full of leads nobody called back fast enough. We can run alongside what you've got or replace it — but we won't duplicate something that's already working.

What actually counts as a qualified estimate?

We agree the criteria in writing before anything goes live: they own the home, they're inside your service area, it's a job you want, their budget fits your pricing, and their timeline works for you.

How quickly can we start?

Usually about a week from the fit call. First booked estimates tend to land inside the fortnight after that.
