Epoxy Flooring · St. Louis, MO
From shared leads and price wars to a 27× return

Grand City Epoxy ran on referrals and Home Advisor leads shared with four or five other contractors at once — a phone-dialing race against price shoppers, with no CRM in sight. With no speed-to-lead and no way to track the pipeline, deals leaked out and revenue plateaued around $18K a month. Since switching to exclusive Facebook leads with 24/7 AI qualification, Jason has closed $115K and lined up $144K more in active proposals in 90 days — off $4,500 in ad spend — and just hired a second installer.
