About

We sell the minute after the lead.

Every contractor we meet is already buying leads from somewhere. Almost none of them have a system for what happens in the sixty seconds after one comes in. That minute decides who gets the job — so that's the business we built.

Why we exist

The pattern we
kept seeing

We didn't set out to work only with contractors. We ran marketing across all kinds of businesses — and the more accounts we ran, the more one thing stood out: nobody was losing because of bad ads. They were losing in the sixty seconds after the lead came in.

The moment it clicked was watching a good contractor lose a job he should have won. The lead was solid, the work was his to take — but he was on site, called back hours later, and the homeowner had already hired the guy who picked up first. The leads weren't the problem. The gap between the click and the callback was. So we built the whole system around closing it: ads to create the demand, a real response in under a minute, a human to qualify, and only ready-to-buy jobs on the calendar.

That's why we're built differently from the agency that sells you a pile of leads and disappears. We don't measure our work in clicks or impressions — we measure it in booked jobs. And we don't take everyone: one contractor per trade, per city, and only the ones we know we can actually move the needle for. If we can't, we'll tell you.
